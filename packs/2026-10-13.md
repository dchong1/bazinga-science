- **B.** Cup1 — too little sugar

### Q5. [STRETCH · match · +3 pt] `ft-s06`

Match the science word to its job in an investigation.

- **A.** IV=deliberate change; DV=measured outcome; Controls=kept constant; Confound=accidental extra change
- **B.** IV=measured outcome; DV=deliberate change; Controls=accidental; Confound=kept constant
- **C.** All four mean 'what you measure'
- **D.** IV and DV are the same thing

_Match pairs (reference):_
- Independent variable ↔ The one factor you deliberately change
- Dependent variable ↔ The outcome you measure
- Control variables ↔ Factors you keep constant
- Confounding variable ↔ An accidental extra change that muddies results

_Answer key (Ada only):_ **A** — Stretch vocab: naming a confound shows you can critique a messy test.
_Science idea:_ Precise variable language = clearer thinking.
